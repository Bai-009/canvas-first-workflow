# CanvasFlow 宣传图

当前宣传图已由用户确认：[英文版](canvasflow-restored-en-matched.png)与[中文版](canvasflow-restored-zh-CN.png)。两版采用中文版所选的轻盈衬线字方向，并从早期原图恢复细腻材质。原图与中间版本保留作过程对照；旧宣传语和此前被否定的材质版本不再使用。

## 表达

项目面向数据处理工作流，目标是通过合适的真实节点能力，将原始数据准备成适合 AI 使用的数据。当前 Demo 验证为此设计的交互与 Agent 架构，尚未接入真实数据处理。

视觉重点是正在发生的协作：一页连续的对话展开成可以逐个进入的节点空间，用户在“切分”节点提出“跨页表格不要切断”，节点内呈现 Agent 的调整状态，关联节点继续形成。纸面承担文字，细蓝线表达关联。首页暂不使用宣传口号。

这是交互方向的概念宣传图，不是应用截图。具体卡片文案用于说明场景，不代表一次已记录的模型调用，也不承诺图中的连线动画已经实现。

沿用现有产品的三枚渐小方块、圆角卡片、蓝色强调与浅色背景；将卡片转译为纸面模型，以具体的材料和空间关系表达画布。字体与标记由生成模型绘制，属于这张概念图，不替换产品内的正式标识。

### 第二版的完整提示词（旧宣传语已弃用）

```text
Use case: ads-marketing. Create an original art-directed conceptual launch image for CanvasFlow. Wide landscape 2:1, high-resolution typography, premium editorial product campaign. Exact text only: "CanvasFlow" and "让想法，在画布上生长。". Keep correct Chinese characters, no extra text.
The memorable metaphor: a flat page of conversation unfolds into a spatial workspace. A long, very thin ivory paper sheet with subtle short typographic grey lines rises from the lower-left, gracefully separates into five individual rounded rectangular paper panels, and fans out across the right two-thirds of the composition. The panels are arranged like an architect's beautiful paper maquette on a cool pale grey studio tabletop, low-relief with precise folds and incredibly fine edges, never chunky plastic blocks. A fine cobalt-blue ink line travels across the surface, connecting the panels; in the nearest largest panel it becomes a small inset local conversation of two restrained blue and grey message shapes. The last part of the blue line softly becomes dashes toward the open right edge. Make the transformation from continuous conversation page to separate connected editable nodes immediately legible. Some panels lie almost flat, two delicately turn upward to catch the light; one turquoise and one warm amber small square are the only secondary colors. Avoid a literal tree, random surreal objects, gadgets, floating glass, glow, futuristic neon, mock browser chrome, or dense UI. This is a concept illustration, not an actual software screenshot.
Composition: strong visual authorship, asymmetric editorial balance; the large dark charcoal CanvasFlow wordmark occupies the upper-left region in a precise modern grotesk sans serif with excellent kerning. The Chinese tagline sits beneath with thoughtful spacing and comfortable scale, not oversized. Three tiny progressively smaller charcoal-to-grey rounded squares accompany the wordmark, echoing the product's existing mark. The paper sculpture reaches the right edge with breathing room. Spacious matte backdrop, bright soft daylight from upper-left, crisp contact shadows, no heavy ambient shadows, no grey fog, no decorative background gradients. Think exquisitely photographed Swiss paper engineering translated into a digital product identity. Clear silhouette, tangible craftsmanship, a single coherent idea rather than an assortment of SaaS symbols. All headline text is flat and legible, never distorted by perspective.
```
