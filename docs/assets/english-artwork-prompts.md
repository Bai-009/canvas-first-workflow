# English banner and wordmark studies

The banner has been localized into English. The user selected a warm, book-like serif direction. The user approved the serif direction, but later rejected the material quality of the [serif banner](canvasflow-collaboration-serif.png). It is superseded by original-source restoration candidates; see [restoration notes](original-source-restoration.md). The lighter sans-serif wordmark is retained as an intermediate proof.

The generated comparison below had unusable contrast and was discarded. The review artifact is [wordmark-study.png](wordmark-study.png), a new typographic specimen rendered directly with locally installed Baskerville Regular, Optima Regular, and Avenir Next Regular at equal cap height. It does not edit the promotional image. No font files are distributed. [wordmark-study.html](wordmark-study.html) provides a local-font HTML comparison as well; browser policy blocked its direct file-URL preview, so the PNG is the inspected deliverable.

All image generation and editing uses the built-in image_gen tool. Existing images are retained.

## Editorial serif direction

[Geist's Anthropic identity case study](https://geist.co/work/anthropic) identifies Styrene and Tiempos as families used in its original identity work. This is a historical reference, not a claim about the current website's exact fonts. The new banner uses generated lettering inspired by that editorial character; no Tiempos font files are embedded or distributed.

The edit changes the upper-left wordmark to a warm editorial serif while retaining the paper workflow scene and English node text. The original banner remains available.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Edit target: the attached CanvasFlow promotional banner.
Change ONLY the CanvasFlow wordmark typography at upper left. Preserve every other element, composition, framing, paper sculpture, shadows, cobalt connections, the three small progressively smaller grey squares, and all English node text.
Replace the very thin geometric sans wordmark with a beautifully drawn warm editorial SERIF wordmark, in the spirit of Anthropic / Claude's literary typographic voice and Tiempos-style book typography. Exact text: "CanvasFlow", same capitalization, one word. Sturdy but graceful medium-weight stems, moderate thick/thin contrast, softly bracketed serifs, generous open counters, a refined double-storey a, lovely C and F, carefully balanced optical kerning. Calm, intelligent, warm, quietly confident. The quality of a finely typeset contemporary literary journal. NOT hairline Didot, not fashion calligraphy, not italic, not generic Times New Roman, not heavy bold. Dark warm charcoal ink. Keep the wordmark approximately the existing footprint, with ample negative space around it. Three grey squares remain aligned near its baseline to the right.
Do not add slogans, captions or new text. Keep the exact existing node text: "Parse", "Chunk", "Keep multi-page tables intact", "Updating chunking strategy", "Embed". Preserve the wide 2:1 composition and bright ivory background.
```
